Consent Test Bench

One section per third-party service. Open DevTools (Network, Application, Console), switch consent on and off in the banner, and watch what loads, what is blocked and what gets written to storage.

Storage inspector

Cookies readable by scripts on this origin, localStorage, sessionStorage, and a log of changes and requests to other domains. Cookies set by iframes on their own domains and HttpOnly cookies are not exposed to page scripts.
